# Tasks: what it should do for the Group Controller (2026-11-12)

Five methods from Ileana Marcut's UX + AI method library, run on the demo's Tasks view (`apps/demo/src/views/Tasks.tsx`) at `main` after #170. The library's AI product principles are the standing quality bar.

Seen in the local build at 800×600, light mode: `?view=tasks` at landing, `?view=tasks&task=credit-limit&at=end`, and `?view=revenue` for comparison. The in-between states were read from `src/lib/close-state.ts`, not watched.

**These are hypotheses.** No research on Tasks exists. The user tests reported on 2026-11-02 covered the channel, not this view. Where a claim rests on an assumption about the user, it says so.

## 1. Quick clarity check

**First read.** A list of four problems AI agents found in the books. One is highlighted and says "Needs your answer". I'd expect to answer it here.

**What's unclear, worst first**

1. **"Needs your answer" doesn't say what the question is.** Neither the row nor the panel holds it. The panel shows rule, evidence and sources, then an outline button, "Open in #revenue", that reads as a way to see more, not as the way to answer.
2. **"Open" on the other three says nothing about whose they are.** Mine to chase, my team's, or the agent's? No owner, no next step.
3. **"Open 4" and "Open" stack.** A badge, then a heading with the same word, then a table. Two labels for one fact, and neither says that only one of the four is mine.
4. **The page is called Tasks and the rows are findings.** A task is something to do. Three of these rows give the reader nothing to do.
5. **The first row is selected before I pick anything.** The tint reads as "you chose this".

**Top 3 fixes**

1. Put the question in the panel, with a primary button that says what it does: "Answer in #revenue".
2. Say whose move each row is, in words.
3. Replace the badge and heading with one line that counts by whose move: "1 needs your answer · 3 open".

## 3. AI control and boundaries review

**The feature.** Four specialist agents each check one rule against named files and raise findings. Tasks lists them. Three kinds of behaviour, kept apart:

- **Automation:** the checks ran at 09:04 and the findings were raised without being asked. Statuses are derived from the store.
- **Suggestion:** in the channel, the agent proposes an entry, and "Choose for me" lets it pick the option.
- **Decision:** where the correction goes, and whether the entry posts. His. The other findings are settled by teammates.

Tasks itself holds automation only. It takes no action and offers none.

**The evaluation**

- **Where should it stop?** It does: nothing in Tasks posts or changes a record, and the agents' profile says they never post an entry, change a locked period, or message outside the channel. Tasks doesn't repeat that boundary, so from here he can't tell what an agent may do with his answer.
- **What should it never do or override?** A status should never be set by an agent's inference. Today "Resolved" comes from a person's action or a rerun check, which is right. Keep it so, and say which.
- **When should it ask instead of assuming?** The hero does: the policy doesn't say whether a locked month reopens, so it asks. Tasks hides that this is why it's asking.
- **When should it say "I don't know"?** It does, in "Not checked", and the self-approved entry says plainly that the limit can't be judged without the employee master. But the row summary carries none of it.
- **What stays with the user?** Reopening a locked period, posting, and whether a finding is real. The first two are gated in the channel. The third has no control anywhere.
- **Where could it mislead?** (a) The row's flat statement, with the caveat a click away. (b) "Nothing open." while a finding waits on Legal. (c) "Open" while the agent is working reads as "nothing is happening".
- **Where might he feel unsure what's happening?** In every state where the move isn't his: three rows at landing, his own row while the agent reads or reruns, and after Skip, when the row reads "Open" with no trace that he skipped.
- **What breaks trust over repeated use?** A list that doesn't separate "yours" from "everyone's" trains him to skim it. A list where a wrong finding can't be dismissed trains him to ignore the agent.
- **Different behaviour for similar inputs?** Can't be judged: the demo is scripted and each agent has one finding.
- **Unhandled edges?** A check that didn't run, a finding with no source left on, two agents raising the same record, a finding that reopens after it was resolved. None exist in the data. See the state inventory.

**Top 3 risks**

1. **He acts on a claim without seeing its caveat.** The row is the claim, and the "not checked" list is in the panel.
2. **He reads the close as clear when it isn't.** "Nothing open." with one Waiting, and "Open" for a skipped question.
3. **He can't correct the agent.** No dismissal, no reason captured, so no feedback loop (principles 5 and 7).

**Improvements**

1. Carry "N not checked" on the row.
2. Count and name every unsettled state in the heading, not only Open.
3. In the panel, state what the answer does and doesn't do: "Nothing posts until you confirm the entry."
4. For the pattern beyond the demo: a "Not an issue" path with a required reason, recorded with his name.

## 5. Eight rough takes

Goal: the Group Controller, mid-close, knows what is his, what blocks sign-off, and gets to it.

1. **Mechanism: decide in place.** The question and its two options live in the panel, and Tasks is where he answers.
2. **Unit: the decision, not the finding.** List only the questions waiting on a person, with findings as the evidence under each.
3. **Grouping: whose move.** Three bands, "Yours", "With the team", "Waiting outside", each row naming who and what next.
4. **Framing: can I sign?** One line at the top: "September can't be signed: 1 answer from you, 1 waiting on Legal until 30 Oct."
5. **Entry point: it comes to him.** New chat opens with "1 needs your answer", and Tasks is the ledger he checks afterwards.
6. **Order: by amount.** One figure column in group currency, largest first, the total at the foot under the double rule.
7. **Time: the close calendar.** Findings against business days and dates: locked on day 6, Legal by 30 Oct.
8. **What it leaves out: the settled.** Only unsettled rows. Resolved ones move to #close's record.

## Conclusion

- **Tasks should answer three questions in this order: what is mine, what else stands between me and sign-off and who has it, and how each one ended.** Today it answers "what did the agents find", which the channels already do.
- **Build takes 3 and 4 together, with the panel half of take 1.** Group rows by whose move, open with one line that counts every unsettled state, and put the question in the panel with a primary "Answer in #revenue". Keep the decision itself in the thread, so there is one place to answer and one record.
- **All of it is in `close.json` already:** `waiting.on`, `resolution.log` and `resolution.by`, `replies`, `notChecked`, and the hero's question and options. No new data, no new component, no new token.
- **Fix three statuses that mislead:** "Open" while the agent works, "Open" after Skip, and "Nothing open." with one Waiting.
- **Put "N not checked" on the row.** It is the product's own principle, and the row is where the claim is made.
- **Leave for later:** an amount column (needs rates the data may not hold), "Not an issue" (out of the script), filters (four rows).
- **Not run: Map needs to AI capabilities.** It needs real user needs from research, and none exist for Tasks. The needs above are read from the role and the script.
- **Settled 2026-11-12:** `PRODUCT.md` named the Head of FP&A as the demo's reviewer. It now names the Group Controller, whom the demo plays.
