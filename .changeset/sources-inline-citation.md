Two product components, built as a pair: `Sources` lists what an answer rests on, numbered, and what wasn't checked (`notChecked` is required, and `[]` says every part was checked), and `InlineCitation` puts a numbered mark after a figure that opens its source and where in it the figure comes from. Both take the same `Source` object, which is exported too.
